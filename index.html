What happened

HealthConnect used a risk register, monthly reviews, and escalation procedures, but key threats were underestimated and mitigation remained reactive. Data migration errors, integration failures, vendor resource shortfalls, and unclear communication reduced clinical visibility and delayed response.

Future-state control model

Engage frontline teams earlier, enforce vendor accountability, and use real-time dashboards with automated escalation. Balance technical measures with stakeholder and clinical-readiness metrics.

Monitoring analysis

Monthly reports were not enough for fast-moving EHR risk.

Control gap

What went wrong

  • No real-time indicators for duplicate records, interface failures, or vendor capacity constraints.
  • Inconsistent escalation left teams managing high-impact issues internally for too long.
  • Technical reporting failed to translate risk into operational effects for clinicians and executives.
Forward controls

What to monitor next

  • Data: Migration error rates, manual cleanup, and legacy documentation.
  • Vendor: Staffing commitments, sprint throughput, and report delivery.
  • Clinical & operational: Readiness, medication workflow safety, uptime, interface stability, and audit readiness.

Executive action plan

Move from predictive-only risk management to adaptive, visible controls.

01Stakeholder Management

Expand Identification

Engage frontline users, physicians, IT, compliance, and vendors through interviews, scenario workshops, checklists, and lessons-learned reviews.

02Data Governance

Audit Early

Mandate data quality assessments, legacy walkthroughs, and proof-of-concept migration before execution.

03Vendor Management

Strengthen Contracts

Require enforceable vendor resource guarantees, capacity reporting, and pre-approved backup support.

04Risk Governance

Automate Escalation

Trigger executive intervention when timeline or budget variance exceeds 10%, or when operational thresholds are breached.

05Incident Response

Create CRIP Coverage

Use an ad hoc response team with the Integration Lead, Security Manager, and IT Director for incidents such as duplicated medical records.
